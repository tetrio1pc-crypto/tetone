CONTROL SETTINGS

DAS — Delayed Auto Shift
Hold time before horizontal auto-repeat starts.
ARR — Auto Repeat Rate
Interval between auto shifts. 0 = instant slide all the way to the wall.
DCD — DAS Cut Delay
Cut time before DAS recharges after a direction change (or when releasing into the opposite held direction).
SDF — Soft Drop Factor
Soft drop speed = current gravity × SDF. Ignored when Instant SDF or Prefer soft drop is ON.
